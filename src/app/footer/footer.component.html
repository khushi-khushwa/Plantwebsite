About Nursery

Welcome to Nursery

For over 25 years, Shyam Nursery has been your trusted partner in nurturing nature and creating beautiful gardens. Located in Sector-62, we are proud to be one of the most established nurseries in the region, offering a wide range of premium garden products and services.

What we offer:
  • Plants:A diverse collection of indoor, outdoor, flowering, and exotic plants.
  • Seeds & Fertilizers:High-quality products to help your plants thrive.
  • Gardening Tools & Accessories:Everything you need for your gardening journey.
  • Landscaping Services:Expert solutions to design and maintain your dream garden.
what make us special
  • Unparalleled Experience: Our team combines years of hands-on gardening expertise to help you make the best choices.
  • Extensive Product Range:From vibrant flowering plants to durable gardening tools, we have it all.
  • Customer-Centric Approach:Whether you’re a beginner or an experienced gardener, we’re here to guide you every step of the way.
  • Sustainability Focus:Every product and service is designed with eco-consciousness in mind.
